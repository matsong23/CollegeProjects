Account Types

Bank

A bank account is exactly what the name suggests, it is an account that is linked to an actual bank account.

Income

An income account is used to track where money is coming in from. You would have multiple of these to track the different ways your company earns money.

Expense

An expense account is used to track where money is going out. You would have multiple of these accounts to track the different ways your company spends money

Accounts Receivable

An accounts receivable account is used to track the amount of money your company is owned by your customers.

Accounts Payable

An accounts payable account is used to track the amount of money your company owes to your vendors and supplies.

Asset

An asset account is used to track things your commpany owns that have value. This could be products, land, vehciles, etc.

Liability

A liability account is used to track what your company owes money on. This could be loans, credit cards, unpayed for goods, etc.
