What is a Transaction?

A transaction is a record of the movement of money between multiple accounts.

What Makes up a Transaction?

A transaction is composed of multiple lines. Each line must have an account and a debit or a credit amount. The debit total and credit total of the transaction must be equal.

How to Read a Transaction

Debit

If a line in a transaction has a debit amount that means that amount of money is moving into the account linked in the transaction line.

Credit

If a line in a transaction has a credit amount that means that amount of money is moving out of the account linked in the transaction line.

Example Types of Transactions

Recording a Bill

This transaction would have lines with an Expense account with a debit amount and a line with a credit amount from a Accounts Payable account.

Paying a Bill

This transaction would have lines with an Accounts Payable account with a debit amount and a line with a credit amount and a Bank account.

Recording an Invoice

This transaction would have multiple lines with an Income acount with a credit amount and a line with a Accouts Receivable account with a debit amount.

Receiving Payment for an Invoice

This transaction would have multiple lines with an Accounts Receivable account with a credit amount and a line with a Bank account with a debit amount.
